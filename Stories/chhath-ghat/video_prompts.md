# छठ घाट का श्राप — Video/Animation Prompts

Style: Cinematic 2D animated horror, slow atmospheric movement, Indian folk horror tone. Use for AI video generation (Kling, Runway, Pika, etc.)

---

## Video 01 — Opening Ghat Atmosphere (5-8s)
Camera slowly pushes forward over a river at dusk, dozens of small oil lamps (diyas) floating on dark water, thick fog curling across the surface, the stone steps of an ancient Indian ghat emerging from the mist, no people, just lamps and fog and silence, slow cinematic camera push, 2D Indian horror animation style

## Video 02 — Chintu Sees the Face (4-6s)
A 7-year-old Indian boy standing on stone steps stares down at dark river water, the water is still, then for a brief flash a pale ghostly woman's face appears just below the surface with eyes open staring up at him, the boy's eyes go wide with terror, his mother grabs his arm and pulls him back, horror animation style, dramatic lighting

## Video 03 — Vikram Walks to Ghat at Night (5-8s)
A lone young Indian man walks left-to-right down a dark village lane at night toward a foggy river, street lamps cast long shadows, a stray dog watches from a doorway, fog thickens ahead where the ghat is, isolated and tense, slow tracking shot following him, 2D horror animation

## Video 04 — Footprints Leading into Water (4-6s)
Phone torch beam sweeps across wet stone ghat steps at night, revealing small barefoot child-sized footprints in the wet stone, the light follows them as they lead straight into the dark river water, no returning footprints, the torch reaches the water's edge and the dark water ripples slightly, close tracking shot, horror atmosphere

## Video 05 — Sumitra Rises from River (6-10s)
Still dark river water at night, a ripple expands from the centre, then the surface breaks as a ghostly woman in white saree slowly rises — not fast, like someone standing up from a bath, long wet black hair, pale glowing skin, arms slightly spread, water streaming off her, she stares directly at camera, eleven oil lamps flicker on the ghat steps in background, slow dramatic rise, 2D supernatural horror

## Video 06 — Woman's Scream and Figure Vanishes (3-5s)
Ghostly white figure standing in river water, a piercing ancient scream echoes, the figure suddenly dissolves into the water like ink dispersing, water splashes and then goes completely still like glass, the ghat is empty, only a fallen torch rolling on wet steps, sudden and shocking, horror animation

## Video 07 — Vikram and Ramesh Walk to Amma's Hut (5-8s)
Two young Indian men walk left-to-right along a riverbank path, afternoon light, yellow mustard fields on one side, dark river on the other, bare winter trees along the bank, a small mud hut with smoke from chimney appears ahead, an ancient woman sits outside facing the river, slow wide tracking shot, atmospheric 2D animation

## Video 08 — The Lullaby Night (5-8s)
Night scene, moonlit fog over a dark river, a vague white shape at the water's edge — it could be a woman standing or just mist — the shape seems to sway gently as if singing, then abruptly it vanishes, the fog closes in, deeply atmospheric and ambiguous, slow push-in camera, horror animation

## Video 09 — Handprints Crawling Out of Water (4-6s)
Morning golden light on wet stone ghat steps, camera slowly reveals small wet handprints — only hands, no feet — coming OUT of the dark water, up two steps, then stopping, fresh lotus flowers sit on the step edge, the camera lingers on the disturbing detail that there are no footprints at all, slow downward tilt reveal, 2D horror

## Video 10 — Flashback: Sumitra's Wedding (4-6s)
Warm golden flashback, a beautiful young Indian woman in red bridal saree walks through a haveli courtyard decorated with marigold garlands and oil lamps, she is smiling, the river sparkles behind her in daylight, everything is warm and happy, a stark contrast to the horror — this is before the tragedy, gentle slow motion, 2D romantic illustration style

## Video 11 — Flashback: The Drowning (8-12s)
Night, 100 years ago. A pregnant woman in white saree prays alone at the ghat, oil lamps floating. Three dark silhouettes approach from behind. Close on her peaceful face. A hand reaches. They push. She falls forward into dark water — splash — cut to underwater: her shocked face, bubbles, hands reaching up. Cut to: surface goes still. The three figures walk away. The lamps keep floating as if nothing happened. Slow, tragic, devastating. 2D horror animation.

## Video 12 — Sumitra's Underwater Transformation (4-6s)
Underwater — a woman's body sinking in dark water, eyes closed, hair floating, then suddenly her eyes snap open glowing white-blue, hair spreads like dark tendrils in every direction, water churns around her, her hands reach upward, supernatural blue-white glow emanates from her, the birth of the Jalmaayi, dramatic underwater horror animation

## Video 13 — Eleven Lamps Ritual (5-8s)
Pitch black ghat at midnight, a young man's hands light oil lamps one by one on stone steps, each flame struggles in the damp air, camera slowly pulls back to reveal eleven points of light arranged on the dark steps with black water beyond, the man kneels at the water's edge holding red glass bangles, ritualistic and powerful, slow cinematic pull-back, 2D horror

## Video 14 — Confrontation: Sumitra Approaches (6-10s)
Ghostly woman (Sumitra) standing in the river facing a young man on the ghat steps, eleven lamps between them, she takes one slow step toward him through the water, her expression is not anger but exhaustion and longing, tears stream from her pale eyes (or is it river water), the lamp flames all lean toward her as she moves, deeply emotional and supernatural, slow dramatic animation

## Video 15 — Amma's Dramatic Entrance (4-6s)
Top of dark ghat steps — an ancient blind woman appears, standing without support, white hair blowing in a wind that doesn't exist, her clouded eyes turned toward the river below, she is impossible — blind, old, alone in darkness — yet she stands with authority, low-angle dramatic shot looking up at her, supernatural atmosphere, 2D horror

## Video 16 — Mother Sees Daughter (6-8s)
Wide shot of ghat — ghostly woman in river looks up at blind old woman at top of steps, eleven lamps between them. The ghost's expression changes — recognition, shock, longing. A tear falls from the old woman's blind eyes. The ghost reaches one translucent hand toward her. The most emotional moment — a mother seeing her daughter for the first time in a hundred years. Slow, devastating, 2D cinematic horror

## Video 17 — Locket Sinks to Sumitra (4-6s)
Close shot — a small tarnished silver locket and red glass bangles are placed gently on the water surface, they float for a moment, then slowly sink into the dark water, a pale translucent hand rises from below to catch the locket, the bangles glow faintly red as ghostly fingers close around them, underwater light, emotional and supernatural, 2D animation

## Video 18 — The Release (6-8s)
Eleven lamp flames simultaneously surge upward — impossibly tall — the entire ghat floods with golden light for one brilliant moment, the ghostly woman in the river closes her eyes and tilts her head back, a long fading scream that turns into the sound of the river, then all eleven flames extinguish at once, total darkness, a massive wave crashes over the lower steps, then — utter stillness, glass-smooth water, 2D dramatic horror animation

## Video 19 — Cliffhanger: Newborn Handprints (4-6s)
Dawn golden light. Camera tilts down to a wet stone step. Fresh tiny handprints appear — much smaller than a child's, like a newborn's — pressed into the wet stone. Camera holds. Then faintly, a child's laugh echoes (not a woman's). The water at the edge of frame ripples once. Something small moves beneath the surface. Cut to black. 2D horror, the final unsettling image.

## Video 20 — Closing: Peaceful Ghat with Hidden Horror (5-8s)
Wide shot. The ghat at dawn, golden peaceful light, Chhath Puja decorations fluttering, women arriving for morning prayers, everything serene and normal. Camera slowly pulls back over the river. Just below the water surface, a small dark shape moves — barely visible. A faint lullaby plays, but in a child's voice. Camera continues pulling back as the ghat shrinks and the dark river fills the frame. 2D atmospheric animation, beautiful and deeply unsettling ending.
